    CAPITAL CAMPAIGN

    Building for the Next 75

    Three projects designed to keep Fox Point East Side Little League strong for the next generation.

    This year, the City of Providence made a significant investment in our fields — a public vote of confidence in this league. 75 Seasons Strong is our answer: a capital campaign to add to that investment and build the infrastructure our next 75 years deserve.

    NEW BATTING CAGES

    Safe. Durable. Ready for What’s Next.

    Our current batting cages were damaged during last winter’s snow storms. This project will replace them with a modern, durable setup that gives players a safe, reliable place to practice, develop their swings, and build confidence.

    Depending on the final footprint and labor costs, the replacing the entire bullpen will cost between $35,000 - 45,000.
